# 0009 — Review resolutions for CC-01

Status: accepted (CC-01). Records how the Codex feasibility review of commit `c3645e0` changed decisions 0001–0008.

Provenance: Codex (Astra) reviewed the PR and applied its corrections directly to the CC-01 working tree, then ran out of usage before posting a written review or this record. Claude (Opus 5.5) reconstructed the findings below from those edits, verified them against the new fixture tests, finished the remaining prose (0006–0009, handoff) and committed the result. The review text itself was not preserved; each finding is stated from the changed specification.

| # | Severity | Finding against `c3645e0` | Resolution | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | blocking | The scalar objective's "bounds" did not prove the claimed priorities. Counter-example: one ALS able to cover all 12 reserve pairs versus a low BLS need at 3600 s — the operating-cost side can reach 15 300, above the 15 000 low unmet penalty, so the solver could withhold service to keep reserve. Waiting cost per need was also unbounded relative to quanta. | Replaced by a lexicographic tuple `(unmet_critical, unmet_high, unmet_medium, unmet_low, waiting_cost, operating_cost)` over demand quanta, solved in passes, each tier fixed only once proven optimal. Timeouts report `lexicographic_complete: false` and `completed_tiers`. Policy bumped to `demo-2026.2`. Regression test covers the counter-example. | 0003, `policy.valid.json`, AS-14, AS-16 |
| R2 | blocking | Idempotency scoped to `(session_id, endpoint, key)` let a retried pre-reset command act on the new session, and a retried reset could reset twice. | Every mutating body carries `expected_session_id` (`409 STALE_SESSION`). Receipts are checked first and persist across resets; reset has a database-wide receipt and returns the same new session on retry. | 0001, 0004, 0005, AS-25, AS-44 |
| R3 | blocking | Per-unit position updates marked as planning-relevant could stale every proposal continuously; debounce could postpone recompute forever. | Raw `UnitPositionObserved` is non-planning. One global `PlanningTickCommitted` per 10 simulated seconds carries positions/locks. Material transitions (lock crossing, breakdown, arrival) commit immediately. Recompute starts ≤ 250 ms after the first material event; a `world_changing` UI state and scenario pause cover sustained churn. | 0005, 0006, AS-28 |
| R4 | blocking | Outbox rows keyed only by plan/assignment could deliver obsolete commands (unit broke down after approval), race between workers, or let a release clear a newer task. | Session-qualified keys, desired-revision fencing, assign/release commands computed against actual execution, `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` re-check at delivery, single terminal transition (`Sent`/`Cancelled`/`Failed`), no side effect before commit. | 0005, AS-45 |
| R5 | blocking | Replay depended on data outside the log (`plan_ref`, seed names, elided events). | `SessionStarted` embeds the initial world and policy; `PlanProposed` embeds the complete plan; triage/assessment events carry resulting facts/needs. Example suffix 53–67 is complete with a checkpoint at 52 (`world.before.json`). | 0005, examples |

Consequential spec changes by Claude while finishing: `inc_0004` is critical under the revised rule table (injury accident with unknown life signs) and 0007 now says so; 0006 gained `world_changing`, `revalidated`, `session_changed` and `database_busy` states; 0007 gained AS-16, AS-25–AS-28 and AS-45.

## Remaining open items (not blocking CC-01)

- Hard-coded 1 s busy budget and 250 ms debounce are unmeasured demo defaults; CC-08/CC-11 measure them.
- Exact-plan repeatability is asserted only for runs that complete every tier and the tie pass; wall-time cutoffs may differ across hardware.
- Comparing unmet quanta across need types (one ALS slot versus one shelter person within a severity tier) is a stated demo simplification.
