Key semantics for Codex:

- Plans and approvals bind to `planning_sequence` (latest event with `affects_planning: true`), not raw head sequence, so audit events (rejected overrides, access logs, outbox queueing) do not make a plan stale. Unit position ticks are coalesced to ≤ 1 per unit per 10 simulated seconds.
- Approval is whole-plan, requires every `requires_ack` flag, and runs in one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction. No auto-approval in the MVP.
- Outbox key `plan_id:assignment_id` doubles as the `SimulatedDispatchSent` idempotency key; replay never runs the sender, solver, router or models.
- Solver: 1 worker, fixed seed, 2 s limit, stable ID tie-break; greedy fallback re-validated; otherwise `failed` and the last approved plan stays in force.
