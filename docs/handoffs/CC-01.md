Key semantics for Codex:

- Plans and approvals bind to `planning_sequence`, not raw head sequence. Raw position telemetry is non-planning; one global `PlanningTickCommitted` per 10 simulated seconds carries planning positions; material transitions commit immediately.
- Every mutating body carries `expected_session_id`; idempotency receipts are checked first and survive resets (`STALE_SESSION`, `DATABASE_BUSY`).
- Approval is whole-plan, requires every `requires_ack` flag, and reads everything inside one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction. No auto-approval.
- Outbox: session-qualified keys, desired-revision fencing, single terminal Sent/Cancelled/Failed; replay never runs the sender, solver, router or models. `SessionStarted` and `PlanProposed` embed full world/plan so the log is self-contained.
- Solver: lexicographic passes, 1 worker, fixed seed, 2 s total budget; incomplete passes reported as such; fallback re-validated; otherwise `failed`.

## Known limitations

- Weights, the 120 s lock, 600 s coverage, 4 s model timeout and question budget are demonstration defaults with no clinical or operational validation.
- Reserve zone polygons and the road fixture are left to CC-03/CC-07.
- No authentication/authorisation model beyond a synthetic operator ID; multi-operator conflicts are handled only by version checks.
- Duplicate-report resolution UI is specified at endpoint level only; detailed screen states belong to CC-10.
- Hindi/Hinglish keyword lists in 0002 are examples, not a complete lexicon (CC-06).
- Busy budget (1 s), debounce (250 ms) and exact-plan repeatability under wall-time cutoffs are unmeasured; cross-type quanta comparison within a tier is a demo simplification.
